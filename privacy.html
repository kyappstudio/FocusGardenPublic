隱私政策

版本 1.2 · 生效日 2026-08-18

一句話

專注花園沒有帳號、沒有分析工具。你的專注紀錄、待辦、標籤與森林都留在你的裝置上。

唯一的例外是免費版底部的橫幅廣告：它由 Google 提供，會連到 Google 的伺服器。訂閱 Premium 之後廣告完全消失，App 就不再發出任何連線。

我們不做什麼

不要求註冊或登入。不讀取通訊錄、位置或麥克風。不內建分析或崩潰回報工具。

不會把你的專注紀錄、待辦、標籤或森林內容傳送給任何人。除了顯示廣告之外，App 不會為了任何其他目的連線。

廣告

免費版在畫面底部顯示一則橫幅廣告，由 Google AdMob 提供。訂閱 Premium 之後廣告不再出現。

廣告是 Google 直接送到你的裝置上的。為了送出廣告，Google 可能會取得你裝置的廣告識別碼、大致的地區與裝置型號等資訊，並依此決定要顯示哪一則。這部分適用 Google 的隱私權政策，你可以在 Android 系統設定的「隱私權 › 廣告」中重設或刪除廣告 ID，也可以關閉個人化廣告。

我們不會把 App 裡的任何內容交給 Google。

App 內另有「看廣告換獎勵」的選項（救活枯樹、試用植物等），一律由你主動點擊才會播放，不看也不影響正常使用。

你的資料存在哪裡

專注紀錄、森林、設定與成就都存在 App 自己的本機儲存空間裡，只有這個 App 讀得到。移除 App 就會一併刪除。

例外：Android 系統本身提供「自動備份」，可能把 App 的資料備份到你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟。那是系統層的功能、由你的 Android 備份設定控制，資料進的是你自己的帳號。你可以在系統設定裡關閉它。

通知權限

用來提醒你專注結束、休息結束，以及專注中離開太久導致樹枯萎。

所有通知都是在你的裝置上排程與顯示的本機通知，不經過任何推播服務，也沒有任何內容離開裝置。

使用情況存取權（選用）

只有在你想使用「允許的 App」這個例外清單時才需要。它讓 App 在專注中判斷你切去了哪個 App，好分辨你是去聽音樂還是去滑社群。

這個判斷完全在你的裝置上完成，判斷結果只用於當下決定樹要不要枯萎；App 不會保存你造訪過哪些應用程式的清單，也不會傳送到任何地方。

不授權完全不影響使用，只是例外清單失效——切到任何 App 都算離開。你可以隨時在系統設定裡收回。

網路權限

網路權限只用於載入廣告。App 的其他功能——計時、紀錄、統計、備份匯出、分享——全部在裝置上完成，不需要連線，也不會連線。

訂閱 Premium 之後廣告停止顯示，App 就完全不再使用這個權限。

匯出與分享

備份檔、成果圖與年度森林圖都是在你按下按鈕時，於裝置上即時產生，再交給系統的分享選單。

檔案要去哪裡完全由你決定。我們不會上傳，也不知道你分享給了誰。

兒童

這個 App 不是為兒童設計的，主要對象是需要專注處理事情的使用者。

我們不蒐集任何人的個人資料，兒童也不例外。廣告的部分請見上方〈廣告〉一節——那是由 Google 提供的，若使用者為兒童，建議由家長在 Android 的家長監護設定中管理。

未來的變更

付費訂閱的交易由 Google Play 處理，App 只會收到「你是否為訂閱者」這項結果，不會接觸你的付款資訊。

政策若有修改，會更新下方的版本與日期。

Privacy Policy

Version 1.2 · Effective 2026-08-18

In short

FocusGarden has no accounts and no analytics. Your focus records, to-dos, tags, and forest stay on your device.

The one exception is the banner ad at the bottom of the free version: it is served by Google and connects to Google's servers. With a Premium subscription the ads disappear entirely and the app makes no connections at all.

What we do not do

No sign-up or login. No access to contacts, location, or microphone. No analytics or crash reporting.

Your focus records, to-dos, tags, and forest are never sent to anyone. Apart from displaying ads, the app makes no connections for any other purpose.

Advertising

The free version shows one banner ad at the bottom of the screen, served by Google AdMob. With a Premium subscription, ads no longer appear.

Ads are delivered by Google directly to your device. To serve them, Google may obtain your device's advertising ID, approximate region, and device model, and use these to decide which ad to show. Google's privacy policy applies to that. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off personalised ads, under Privacy › Ads in Android settings.

We never hand any in-app content to Google.

The app also offers optional "watch an ad for a reward" actions (reviving a withered tree, trying a plant). These only play when you tap them, and declining changes nothing else.

Where your data lives

Focus records, your forest, settings, and achievements are stored in the app's own local storage, readable only by this app. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

One exception: Android itself offers Auto Backup, which may back up app data to your own Google Drive. That is a system-level feature controlled by your Android backup settings, and the data goes to your own account. You can turn it off in system settings.

Notification permission

Used to remind you when a focus session ends, when a break ends, and when a tree is withering because you left the app.

All notifications are local, scheduled and shown on your device. No push service is involved and nothing leaves the device.

Usage access (optional)

Only needed if you want to use the "allowed apps" exception list. It lets the app tell which app you switched to during a focus session, so it can distinguish listening to music from scrolling social media.

The check happens entirely on your device and the result is used only to decide, at that moment, whether the tree withers. The app does not keep a list of which apps you visited, and never transmits it.

Declining changes nothing except that the exception list stops working — switching to any app counts as leaving. You can revoke it at any time in system settings.

Internet permission

The internet permission is used only to load ads. Everything else — timing, records, statistics, backup export, sharing — happens on your device, needs no connection, and makes none.

With a Premium subscription, ads stop and the app no longer uses this permission at all.

Export and sharing

Backups, result cards, and the year-in-forest image are generated on your device the moment you tap the button, then handed to the system share sheet.

Where the file goes is entirely your choice. We never upload it and have no knowledge of who you share it with.

Children

This app is not designed for children; it is aimed at people who need to focus on getting things done.

We collect no personal data from anyone, children included. For advertising, see the Advertising section above — that is provided by Google, and where the user is a child we recommend parents manage it through Android's parental controls.

Future changes

Subscription transactions are handled by Google Play. The app receives only whether you are a subscriber, never your payment details.

Any revision updates the version and date below.
